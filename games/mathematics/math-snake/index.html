Math Snake

Eat the bubble that equals the answer.

  • Eat the correct answer to grow and score. A longer combo is worth more.
  • A wrong answer still grows you by one cell and subtracts 5 points. The score stays at least 0.
  • Eat the bubble marked P to pass through walls 3 times.
  • Hit a wall or yourself and the game ends.

Desktop: WASD or the arrow keys. Phone and tablet: swipe. You cannot reverse, and each step accepts one turn.
